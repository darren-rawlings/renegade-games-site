Questions

Are the games free?

Yes. Every game is free to download and play. There's an ad between games now and then; you can remove all ads from a game with an optional one-time purchase in its Settings. You can also leave a tip there if you'd like to support us.

Are they suitable for children?

Yes. The games are for all ages, and they follow Google Play's Families policy. Ads are set up for children: only family-friendly ads, never personalised. Purchases go through Google Play's own screen, which asks for a password or a parent's approval.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The games play offline. Ads only appear when you're connected.

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for. Your settings are saved on your phone.

Which phones do they run on?

Android phones, through Google Play. They're built for phones held upright.

When can I get them?

The games are being tested now and will arrive on Google Play soon. Each game's button on this page will link straight to it.

I found a problem, or have an idea.

We'd love to hear it. Email support@renegade-games.com and tell us which game and what happened.
