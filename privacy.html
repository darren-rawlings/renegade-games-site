Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026. Applies to all Renegade Games apps, including Counter Fire: Four in a Row.

The short version

Our games don't ask for your name, email, location or any other personal information, and we don't have accounts or in-app purchases. Our games are free and show ads from Google AdMob, set up for children: only family-friendly ads, never personalised.

Information we collect

None that identifies you. Your game settings (such as board style and sound on or off) are saved only on your device and are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Because our games are for all ages, including children, we tell AdMob to treat every ad request as directed to children. That means:

  • ads are not personalised and are not based on your interests or past activity;
  • only ads suitable for general audiences (rated "G") are shown;
  • your device's advertising ID is not used to target ads at you.

To deliver ads and prevent fraud, Google may still process limited technical information, such as the device type, operating system version and IP address. How Google handles this is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In some regions (such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) you may be asked for consent before ads are shown, as the law requires.

Children's privacy

Our games are suitable for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child's information has been collected through one of our games, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
