Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026. Applies to all Renegade Games apps, including Counter Fire: Four in a Row.

The short version

Our games don't ask for your name, email, location or any other personal information, and there are no accounts. Our games are free and show ads from Google AdMob, set up for children: only family-friendly ads, never personalised. You can remove the ads, or leave a tip, with an optional purchase through Google Play.

Information we collect

None that identifies you. Your game settings (such as board style and sound on or off) are saved only on your device and are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Because our games are for all ages, including children, we tell AdMob to treat every ad request as directed to children. That means:

  • ads are not personalised and are not based on your interests or past activity;
  • only ads suitable for general audiences (rated "G") are shown;
  • your device's advertising ID is not used to target ads at you.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ads software in the game still collects and shares with Google some technical information: your IP address (which gives a rough idea of location), how the app and its ads are used (for example app launches, taps and ads viewed), information about how the app is performing, and an app-specific device identifier (the app set ID). This information is encrypted in transit. How Google handles it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In some regions (such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) you may be asked for consent before ads are shown, as the law requires.

Purchases

"Remove ads" and tips are optional one-time purchases made through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment using your Google account; we never see your name, account or payment details, only whether a purchase was made, and that is stored on your device. Google Play's own purchase screen asks for your password or a parent's approval, depending on your settings.

Children's privacy

Our games are suitable for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child's information has been collected through one of our games, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
